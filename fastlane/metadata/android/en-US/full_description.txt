OpenSatFinder turns your phone into the instrument you use to aim a satellite dish.

Choose a satellite from the built-in catalogue of geostationary satellites. The compass
shows where the phone is pointing and where the satellite sits; the arc underneath shows
the elevation, the angle above the horizon, for the phone and for the satellite. Turn and
lift until the screen goes green: that is within two degrees of where the dish has to
look. Two tones, one for the bearing and one for the elevation, let you aim without
watching the screen.

• Compass corrected for magnetic declination, from your position
• Elevation gauge: the phone's tilt against the satellite's own elevation
• Every geostationary satellite CelesTrak lists, searchable, ordered by elevation
• Favourites, and a setting that hides the satellites below the horizon
• Audible cues in five volume steps
• Eight languages: Catalan, English, Basque, French, Galician, German, Portuguese,
  Spanish

Privacy: the position never leaves the phone. The app has no network permission at all:
no account, no tracking, no advertising and nothing to buy.
